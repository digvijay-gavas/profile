About

I'm a mechanical engineer by training and a software engineer by trade — a combination that has quietly shaped everything since. A Master's in Mechanical Engineering (Thermal) gave me an instinct for how physical products come together; a decade inside Teamcenter and the PLM stack taught me how that same product data moves, breaks, and gets stitched back together across CAD, BOM, and change-management systems.

Since 2016 I've worked across the full PLM lifecycle — as a Teamcenter admin, a DevOps engineer wiring up CI/CD for enterprise deployments, and a senior developer building customizations in ITK C++, Active Workspace React, and T4EA integrations for automotive, aerospace-adjacent, and certification clients across Germany, the UK, and India.

These days that same curiosity points at AI in the PLM domain — applying LLMs and automation to the unglamorous, high-value problems of product data: parsing, migration, document intelligence, and connecting engineering systems that were never designed to talk to each other. Outside of work, I'm the person prototyping a robot arm on a weekend or writing a Python script to generate 3D-printable geometry for a physicist's density field, purely because it's fun.
