World Could Be a Better Place

Published Sep 3, 2026 · Handwritten, transcribed as-is with spelling only corrected

My motive to earn money is to change the world to be a better place. Do you remember that tubelight, that can be controlled by smartphone app, you have lots of setting, you can set any color, any brightness; you can even automate its color brightness across day, wow, amazed by it. But—

Yes ‘but’, always comes. I realised when wifi is down you cannot do anything of it. You stuck, its even frustrating that app login suddenly fail and you have to relogin. You won’t remember the password. You reset and you forget to save password or even you don’t have even option to save it. Nasty things.

Being technology enthusiast, creator I understand these things from how they are build. I realize why they are made the way it is.

The company making them have interest to keep them online, one to track, when and how you are using it. And second to lock the functionality down, by asking new purchase, app upgrade or subscription fees. They are capitalist, making more money with less — it is their goal.

We feel so locked out, we don’t own the thing we brought. One more question from other side is, have we paid enough to own it. We have got it at subsidised price, because company is getting remaining money later on, by selling your data to advertisement company, they know when you sleep, when you wake up just by your tubelight you bought. They know when did you worked late night because you have to deliver something to someone.

Why we fall in place we are, was these revenue model are deliberately imposed on us. I believe thats downside of capitalism, its organic to these things emerged if capitalism is not deliberately controlled.

Being individual contributor to the world, I want to fix these things. The question is ‘can I’. Do I really have enough power, motivation to fix those things. I see people develop solutions to fight these bad practices, but the solutions developed by individuals are not commercialised, only who goes into deep can understand the solution and use it. Take another example, there is something called as Pi-hole, its raspberry-pi based local domain server for internet control. It designed to block the ad, data brokers domain. It exist, it works, but its hard for any common people to use it. Its never became mainstream product, that people can buy of the shelf.

I feel like losing hope, I can’t fix it. But recently I believe there is hope. I see more people are aware of it, more people talk about it, more people want the change. I think its not me who need to fix it, but the everyone together.

World will be better place
we will make it together Digvijay Gavas
